v0.2.0 — Themes, fonts, and a lot more

NEW
• Pick from 5 color themes (Indigo, Forest, Sunset, Ocean, Graphite). Each is tuned for both light and dark mode.
• Switch between System, Light, and Dark from Settings.
• Choose your reading font: System, Rounded, Charter, Monospace, iA Writer, or JetBrains Mono.
• Editor and Preview font sizes are independently adjustable.
• Recent files appear on the Home screen.
• Insert images directly into your markdown via the photo picker. On Save, images are copied next to your .md file so they render in any other markdown app too.
• Export any document as a PDF.
• Filename shows in the nav bar. Tap the menu to rename in place.

POLISHED
• Floating glass toolbar in the editor with larger touch targets and a working hide-keyboard button.
• Single-icon mode toggle: tap the eye to read, pencil to write.
• Swipe from the left edge to dismiss any open document.
• Settings shortcut from inside any document's menu.
• Softer light/dark home palette.

PRIVACY
Markflow continues to make zero network requests and collect no data. This release ships with the App Store privacy manifest.
